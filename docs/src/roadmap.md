These are **plans, not promises** — nothing below is shipped in v0.15.0.

- ~~**Real e-signature**~~ — shipped in v0.3.0: typed-name / drawn-signature capture on the contract, stored with the project, SHA-256 tamper evidence.
- ~~**Late-fee accrual calculator**~~ — shipped in v0.4.0: monthly-compounding accrual of contract §3 fees, `invoice late-fees` CLI, dashboard rows, dunning template variables.
- ~~**SMS / WhatsApp escalation**~~ — shipped in v0.5.0: day-15 also goes out as an SMS/WhatsApp message via Twilio when configured, plus `invoice notify` for one-off reminders.
- **Agency mode** — multi-freelancer workspaces (unshipped); per-client dunning policies shipped in v0.7.0 (`invoiceguard client set-dunning-policy`); freelancer roster + invoice attribution shipped in v0.8.0 (`invoiceguard freelancer add`, `invoiceguard invoice assign`); freelancer earnings shipped in v0.9.0 (`invoiceguard freelancer show`, per-currency billed/collected/outstanding); freelancer payouts shipped in v0.10.0 (`invoiceguard payout record` / `payout list`, per-currency payout ledger with an over-payout guard); revenue splits shipped in v0.11.0 (`freelancer add --split-pct` / `freelancer set-split`, per-currency expected share + still-due-to-freelancer); payout suggestions shipped in v0.12.0 (`invoiceguard payout suggest`, per-currency still-due share or still-held balance when no split is set); payout settlement shipped in v0.13.0 (`invoiceguard payout settle`, records every suggestion at once through the payout guard, no-split freelancers skipped, `--dry-run` writes nothing). agency summary shipped in v0.14.0 (`invoiceguard agency summary`, whole-book view per freelancer per currency with per-currency totals, read-only); payout schedules shipped in v0.15.0 (`freelancer set-schedule` / `clear-schedule`, `invoiceguard payout schedule` with derived next payout date, read-only); payouts due shipped in v0.16.0 (`invoiceguard payout due`, scheduled freelancers with an actionable payout on/inside the pay-run window, read-only); settle the pay run shipped in v0.17.0 (`invoiceguard payout settle-due`, one payout per due row through the payout guard, no-split freelancers skipped, `--dry-run` writes nothing, second run is a no-op); payouts overdue shipped in v0.18.0 (`invoiceguard payout overdue`, scheduled freelancers whose most recent payout date passed with an actionable payout and nothing recorded since that date, read-only); settle the missed pay runs shipped in v0.19.0 (`invoiceguard payout settle-overdue`, one payout per overdue row through the payout guard, no-split freelancers skipped, `--dry-run` writes nothing, second run is a no-op); payout timing on the agency summary shipped in v0.20.0 (`invoiceguard agency summary [--as-of DATE]`, per-currency due/overdue/upcoming/settled/no-schedule status with next or missed payout date, derived from the `payout due`/`overdue` rules, read-only); payout timing on the agency summary TOTALS shipped in v0.21.0 (`reports.agency_summary_totals()`, per-currency rollup of the rows' own statuses — overdue beats due beats upcoming, with freelancer count and earliest missed/next date; a settled/no-schedule mix carries no status rather than a misleading one, read-only); payout timing on `freelancer show` shipped in v0.22.0 (`invoiceguard freelancer show ID [--as-of DATE]`, per-currency due/overdue/upcoming/settled/no-schedule status with next or missed payout date, taken from the same `agency summary` rows, read-only); payout timing on `payout suggest` shipped in v0.23.0 (`invoiceguard payout suggest [--as-of DATE]`, per-suggestion due/overdue/upcoming/no-schedule status with next or missed payout date, derived from the `payout due`/`overdue` rules, read-only); payout timing on `payout schedule` shipped in v0.24.0 (`invoiceguard payout schedule [--as-of DATE]`, per-freelancer due/overdue/upcoming/settled/no-schedule status rolled up across currencies with the same overdue-beats-due-beats-upcoming precedence, read-only). payout timing on `freelancer list` shipped in v0.25.0 (`invoiceguard freelancer list [--as-of DATE]`, the same per-freelancer rollup appended to each roster line, read-only); payout timing on `invoice report` shipped in v0.26.0 (`invoiceguard invoice report [--as-of DATE]`, per-currency due/overdue/upcoming/settled/no-schedule status on each 'by freelancer' line, taken from the same `agency summary` rows, read-only). payout timing on `payout list` shipped in v0.27.0 (`invoiceguard payout list [--as-of DATE]`, the same per-freelancer rollup on each freelancer's newest payout row, read-only).
- ~~**Partial payments / payment plans**~~ — shipped in v0.2.0.

Priorities follow real user feedback. If a roadmap item matters to you, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Bilal-Junaid-Jiwani/invoiceguard) describing your workflow.
